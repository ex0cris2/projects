Why Nusa Dua is the right base in December

Most Bali beaches get murky water and washed-up debris in the wet season. Nusa Dua keeps clear water and is the island's go-to rainy-season beach. Everything on the Bukit peninsula is inside 40 minutes; Ubud and Canggu are the only long hauls.

Free, and walkable from the hotel

Water Blow on Peninsula Island — waves funnel through a cliff gap and shoot up to 30 m. Free, parking only, best at mid-to-high tide.
Nusa Dua Promenade — paved 7 km coastal walkway.
Pantai Mengiat and Geger — public beaches despite the resorts.
Puja Mandala — five houses of worship side by side, 10 minutes away.

How bad is Bali traffic, really

Bad enough to wreck a plan. Nusa Dua to Ubud is 45 km and takes 1.5–2 hours, up to 2.5 in traffic. Canggu is 38 km and can take 2.5 hours. Uluwatu's narrow roads clog before sunset — a 20-minute hop can stretch to 40 or 50. Leave early, and never schedule two far-apart places in one day.

Tourist levy Rp 150,000 each

Every foreign visitor pays Rp 150,000 () once per entry, separate from any visa. Pay on the official Love Bali portal before flying and keep the QR voucher. Children and infants pay too. Visitors who have not paid can be refused entry.

Three levy scams were still running in 2026 — only ever pay on the official portal, never a QR code handed to you at the airport.

Places

Souvenirs, ink, coffee and somewhere dry when it pours. Drive times are from the hotel.

Souvenirs on site → 1h

Bali Collection — in the Nusa Dua resort complex, so no drive at all. Padi Art Market inside has crafts, shell work, herbs and spices sourced from Gianyar craftsmen; Luluca, Alun Alun Indonesia and Sikut Satak are in the same square, plus a duty-free store. ITDC Complex, Nusa Dua

Krisna Oleh-Oleh — Southeast Asia's largest souvenir chain, fixed prices so no haggling, and a food court. Coffee, pie susu, textiles, paintings. Branches on Jl. Raya Tuban in Kuta (35 min) and Sunset Road. Best single stop if you're buying for everyone back home.

Ubud Art Market — free entry, bargaining expected, only worth it on an Ubud day. Erlangga 2 in Denpasar and The Keranjang Bali are the other big one-stop shops.

Tattoos read this first

There are roughly 280 studios on the island and quality varies a lot. Seminyak (50 min) has the strongest hygiene and international credentials; Canggu (1.5–2.5h) is the densest, with many artists trained in Europe or Australia; Kuta (35 min) has the most walk-ins and the widest quality spread.

Names that come up repeatedly: Artful Ink, Mason's Ink (Australian-overseen, hospital-grade equipment), Angel Ink, Seminyak Ink, Canggu Ink Club, Amor Fati.

Check before you sit down: needles unsealed in front of you, single-use only, named-brand ink bottles clearly labelled. Sterile packs opened early or mystery inks mean walk out. Allow healing time before swimming — that's a real conflict with a beach trip.

Coffee 5–30 min

In Nusa Dua: Kopi Petani, 25:PM Coffee, Patia Coffee, DUA Kafe (Jl. Pantai Mengiat), Rehat Cafe, Honey and Bread (in the Pepito building), Secret Cafe. Expect coffee at 25,000–50,000 and mains from 40,000.

Jimbaran, 25 min: Mövenpick Café (Jl. Wanagiri No. 1, straight off the main road), Bull's Coffee, Teraskota Coffee & Eatery.

When it rains indoor

Close by: a Balinese massage or spa afternoon — the default rainy-day move, and cheap. GWK's indoor museum floors (9th and 23rd) stay dry. Bali Collection for shopping and a meal.

Worth the drive: Trans Studio Bali, an indoor theme park in Denpasar, tickets from 500,000 () — busy on rainy days. Beachwalk Shopping Center in Kuta. Icon Bali Mall in Sanur, which has an indoor floating market. Cinema XXI from 40,000 ().

Make something: Balinese cooking class, silver jewellery making in Ubud, Mason Chocolate's factory workshop near Ubud, pottery, or Hatten Wines' Cellardoor tasting in Sanur. For a group: escape rooms in Sanur, or axe throwing at Finns Recreation Club in Canggu.
